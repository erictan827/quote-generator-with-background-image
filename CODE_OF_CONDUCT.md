# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, acceptance of differing viewpoints, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what benefits the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized attention, threats, publication of private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository owner through an appropriate contact channel shown on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate.
